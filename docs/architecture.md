## Three data tiers (the demo cannot go blank)
1. Live: `GET /api/bundle`, cached in `localStorage` with a timestamp.
2. Cache: last good bundle, shown with a "saved at" banner.
3. Snapshot: `frontend/src/data/fallbackBundle.json`, generated by `scripts/sync_fallback.py`.

## Trust rules (services/trust.py)
Five visible checks: source, link, freshness (3 h / 12 h thresholds), corroboration (linked hazard reports, or contradiction), data origin. Levels: `official`, `simulated_official`, `unverified`, `expired`.

## Risk rules (services/risk.py)
`danger`: inside a reported flood, river flood or sea-surge area. `caution`: within 1.5 km of one, inside or near a road closure, or inside the projected cyclone zone. `watch`: otherwise. Unverified claims never change the level. Shelters inside flood areas are listed last.

## Honest limits
- All alerts, hazards and shelters are simulated. Swap `backend/app/data/*.json` for a real, licensed feed to go live.
- Tamil, Hindi and Telugu strings are demo translations and need review by native speakers before real use.
- Distances are straight-line, not routes. Shelter availability is shown as open only when the record carries a recent verification time.
- Trust check details are English only; check names and statuses are translated.
